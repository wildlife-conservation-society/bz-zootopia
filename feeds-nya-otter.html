Sea Otter Cam

Southern sea otters (Enhydra lutris nereis), also known as California sea otters, are native to the coast of California. The New York Aquarium is proud to provide a home for rescued sea otters, including our three current residents.

The camera is live daily from 10am to 4pm EST.

What happened to the exhibit panel? One of our otters got a little too excited playing with a rock (a totally natural behavior!) and cracked the inner layer of the viewing glass. The glass is mulit-layered and secure, and everything is safe for the otters as we work on future repairs.
