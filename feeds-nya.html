Sea Otters

Southern sea otters (Enhydra lutris nereis), also known as California sea otters, are native to the coast of California.The New York Aquarium has provided a home for 11 rescued sea otters since 1991, and the aquarium’s Sea Cliffs exhibit is a great place to watch these aquatic mammals in action!
